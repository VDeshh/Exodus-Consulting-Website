Why this page exists

A build in progress is not a launch, and we will not write it as one.

Most consulting portfolios flatten everything into one tense, so a system that went live last year reads the same as one that is still being written. Ours does not. Six engagements sit across this practice: two live, one shipped and three in build, every one of them real work with a client. The two flagship studies have their own pages, and this page holds the other three so the difference between running and being built stays visible.

ABC is live, and what we describe is the work we do rather than any outcome at the ballot box. B-Side Group and Matchbox Global are under construction: real work, no launch metric yet. Where there is no number, we say so instead of borrowing one.

Engagement 01

ABC Live

ABC is a municipal political party in Vancouver. We run their data consolidation and assist with their targeted marketing campaigns: bringing the party's own data into one place, then supporting their campaign outreach with it.

Consolidation comes first because it has to. An organisation that has grown in stages ends up holding its information in whatever tool each part of the operation happened to adopt, and marketing work built on top of that spends its budget reconciling records instead of reaching people. One consolidated view is the cheaper foundation, and it is the part we own.

It is live. We claim no electoral or polling outcome here, we take no position on the party or its policies, and nothing on this page should be read as the party endorsing us. What we are accountable for is the consolidation and the campaign marketing support, and that is the whole of what we describe.

Engagement 02

B-Side Group In build

B-Side Group is a hospitality group. We are building them customised inventory management software across three of their restaurant venues.

Inventory is where a multi-venue group loses money quietly. Each room counts its own stock its own way, the same product carries three names across three sites, and the gap between what was bought and what was sold only surfaces at the end of the month, by which time it is history rather than a decision. Software of this kind is our answer to that: stock counts done per site, one catalogue shared across the venues, purchased against sold set side by side as variance, and reorder points that fire off real consumption rather than a hunch.

It is in build, so read that paragraph as the shape of the build and not as a delivered result. No count is running in our software yet, no variance figure exists, and there is no launch metric or screenshot to show. When there is one, it will appear with its arithmetic next to it, the same as everything else on this site.

Engagement 03

Matchbox Global In build

Matchbox Global is a UK matchmaking platform. We are building the technology layer: taking complicated matching logic and turning it into software that stays clean as it scales, and moving the product from architecture toward production.

Matching is the whole product, so the logic is the whole risk. Rules that read fine on a whiteboard turn into branching special cases the moment real people and real preferences arrive, and that is what gets rewritten at scale. Our job is to make that layer explicit and testable before the platform depends on it.

It is in build. There is no launch metric to quote, no screenshot to show and no result to claim yet. When there is one, it will appear with its arithmetic next to it, the same as everything else on this site.
