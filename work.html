How to read this page

A build in progress is not a finished one. A projection is not a result.

Most consulting portfolios flatten everything into one tense. Ours does not. Every entry carries a tag, and the tag is the truth about where that work stands today. Clients are named wherever we are able to name them, and where we are not, the sector is named instead. Where the only number available is a forecast, it is labelled PROJECTION and the arithmetic is printed so you can check it.

What the tags mean

Live
Running now. Either a product in production or work we are accountable for today.
Shipped
Built, delivered, in the client's hands. The engagement is closed.
In build
Under construction. Some phases are delivered, some are not. We say which.
Projection
A forecast from the client's own numbers. Not a booked result and never presented as one.

What you will not find here

No logo wall with nothing behind it. No percentage without the sum that produced it. No screenshot of a product that does not exist. Where we have no capture of a working surface, the study draws an interface diagram and labels it as a diagram.

Every screenshot on this site is a real capture from a live product, cropped only to remove guest names, emails, phone numbers and pass IDs.

Uniserve Communications

In build

Uniserve is a Canadian internet and telecom provider. They brought us in for a three month AI enablement programme aimed at the high volume of repeat customer questions arriving through their support channels. Phase 1 mapped where support time was going and shortlisted the places AI would actually pay. Phase 2 is in build now: a customer-facing chatbot and a voice agent for residential customer support, with data and models hosted in Canada.

Projection $10,000

per technician per year, from roughly one hour a day given back: 1 hr/day x about 250 working days x $40/hr. A forecast from Uniserve's own numbers, not a booked result.

Live

Palapa Tours Ottawa

Shipped

Palapa Tours runs floating tiki-bar cruises, and the problem was not revenue, it was the tip-out. Gratuity splits shifted cruise to cruise and were hard to reconcile fairly, so payouts were argued over after the boat came in. We built custom tip-out software that calculates the pool, applies the split rules and tracks staff gratuities automatically. The engagement is closed and the software is theirs.

No metric was published on this one. What changed is that the same rules now run on every sailing, so the disputes stopped.

B-Side Group

B-Side Group

In build

B-Side Group is a hospitality group, named here with their consent. We are building them customised inventory management software across three of their restaurant venues. Software of this kind is where our inventory work usually lands: stock counted per site, one catalogue shared across the venues rather than a separate list per room, the variance between what was bought and what was actually sold, and reorder points that fire before a line runs out.

That is what is being built, not what has been delivered. No metric is published on this one and none is claimed while the build is open.
