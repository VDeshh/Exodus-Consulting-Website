22 industries. One method.

The way we build does not change: learn the operation first, find the one workflow worth fixing, agree the number in writing, then ship software the team actually uses. What changes per industry is the systems of record, the rules, and the failure that costs the most. Each page below is written for one sector, and says which.

What stays the same

The method does not change when the industry does.

The first week of every engagement is spent on the floor rather than in a repository: we learn the job the way the team does it, in their words, on their screens. Then we build the piece that removes the part they hate, and we stay until they use it without us.

Your systems of record stay where they are. We read them and write back only where you have said we can, and anything irreversible arrives as a draft with a named reviewer.

The audit comes first
Three to five days mapping where the hours and the money actually go, ending in a ranked plan with the payback math. Yours to keep, whoever builds it.
The number is agreed in writing
You sign off on the baseline before the build starts. The clock starts at deployment, not at signature.
A person stays in the loop
Anything that spends money, sends something irreversible or carries a professional obligation is a draft until someone approves it.
You own it
The code and the data are yours, written into the agreement before anything is built.

Where we have built

Where we have built

The industries our own engagements sit in. Each page names the work and what it taught us.

  1. 01

    Nightlife and hospitality

    A venue running five tools is running five versions of Saturday.

    Pour reconciled to sales, per bartender. Bluetooth scale weigh-in at close. One guest record from every ticket seller

  2. 02

    Restaurants and bars

    The money leaks between what you bought and what you sold.

    Poured against sold, per bar, per shift. One catalogue, every site, one count. Reorder points that draft the order

  3. 03

    Event venues and ticketing

    Keep selling where you sell. Own the guest yourself.

    Every ticket buyer lands in your CRM. A door scanner that works with no signal. Promoter portal with a live commission ledger

  4. 04

    Telecom and internet providers

    The repeat question is the biggest cost in your support queue.

    Voice agent for residential support. Website chatbot on your own plans and policies. Outage-aware answers on the worst day

  5. 05

    Political parties and campaign organisations

    A campaign runs on lists, and the lists disagree.

    Every source read into one shape. Records matched and every join scored. A review queue for the near-misses

  6. 06

    Tour and experience operators

    Every departure is a shift, a manifest and a payout.

    Tip-out engine per departure. Voice agent for the booking phone. Website chatbot on the operator's own pages

Where the same build applies

Where the same build applies

Different rules, different systems of record, the same method. What we would build, and how it works.

  1. 01

    Law firms

    Three billable hours in eight, and the phone goes unanswered.

    A voice agent that answers new client calls. A website assistant for the questions that repeat. Intake, conflict check and client ID in one flow

  2. 02

    Accounting and bookkeeping firms

    Every firm sells hours it spends chasing documents.

    Document chasing that runs itself. Slip and receipt extraction with confidence scores. Uncategorised transactions, batched and asked once

  3. 03

    Healthcare clinics

    Most clinic hours go to the phone, not the patient.

    Voice agent for the routine calls. Website chatbot that shrinks the email queue. Fax and referral intake with confidence scores

  4. 04

    Recruiting and staffing

    Your recruiters were hired to place people, not chase timesheets.

    A voice agent on the candidate line. A website chatbot for candidates and clients. Candidate records reconciled with confidence scores

  5. 05

    Professional services and agencies

    Your firm sells hours, and nobody writes them all down.

    Voice agent on the main line. Website chatbot for the repeat questions. Timesheets drafted from work already done

  6. 06

    Insurance and financial brokers

    A broker's day is mostly retyping what a system already knows.

    A voice agent on the office line. A chatbot on your own documents. eDocs and statements parsed to the policy

  7. 07

    Real estate and property management

    The rent roll is right. Everything around it is retyped.

    Voice agent on the tenant line. Website chatbot for listing and tenant questions. Maintenance intake, triage and dispatch

  8. 08

    Construction and trades

    The job is won on the phone and lost on paperwork.

    Voice agent that answers on site days. Website chatbot for scope and quote questions. Timesheets reconciled to jobs and payroll

  9. 09

    Home services and trades

    Every unanswered call is a job the next company books.

    A voice agent that answers every call. A website chatbot for the repeat questions. A dispatch board that proposes the day

  10. 10

    Logistics and transportation

    Every load gets typed in three times before it is paid.

    Voice agent for check calls and quotes. Rate confirmation and BOL parsing. Detention and accessorial recovery

  11. 11

    Manufacturing and distribution

    The ERP holds the parts. The answers still live in inboxes.

    Voice agent for order status calls. Website chatbot for spec questions. Purchase orders parsed and matched

  12. 12

    Retail and ecommerce

    The shop, the web store and the books never agree on stock.

    One stock number across every channel. Reorder proposals from real sell-through. Website chatbot for orders, stock and returns

  13. 13

    Automotive dealers and service

    A dealership holds one customer in six systems that never reconcile.

    Voice agent on the service line. Website chatbot for the repetitive questions. One customer record across every system

  14. 14

    Fitness and wellness

    Your billing system knows who pays, not who is leaving.

    Voice agent on the front desk line. Website chatbot for the repeat questions. Intake, waiver and consent capture

  15. 15

    Education and training providers

    The enquiry, the enrolment and the invoice live in different systems.

    Voice agent on the admissions line. Chatbot on the programme pages. Enquiry follow-up that drops nothing

  16. 16

    Nonprofits and associations

    Your donors exist three times, in three systems, spelled three ways.

    Voice agent on the main line. Website chatbot for the repeat questions. One supporter record across every system
